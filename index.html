Research in a Stick (RIS)

USB stick that runs AI offline. No internet, no cloud, no install. Plug in, open a browser, get chat, knowledge library, document analysis, and citation formatting in English and Kiswahili. Built for students and researchers the internet misses.

Kabarak Smart Attendance (KSAS)

QR codes that refresh every five seconds. Device fingerprint, one-time tokens, optional GPS and campus IP. Screenshots of old codes fail by design. Role dashboards for students, lecturers, admins, HODs, and deans.
