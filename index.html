How the work gets made

Constraint first. Product second. Stack last.

React or Next when bandwidth exists; Node, Express, Postgres on USSD and money paths; FastAPI for research corpora; Firebase when realtime ops matter more than elegance. Thin client, boring API, offline path.

Law school taught me to read the rule. Field ops taught me to build the one that survives. I organize robotics and applied AI workshops on campus while leading Research in a Stick.
